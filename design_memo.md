# Design Memo — NimbusCare Policy-Grounded Workflow

An offline, deterministic workflow routes by the full question, product category, requested action, and case facts. Its evidence ledger records ranked retrieval and reasoned exact-ID fetches with source file, line range, excerpt, and transcript provenance. Claims use only logged passages. `Decimal` and calendar dates control money and deadlines. User text cannot change rules, trigger account actions, or establish missing facts.

For Dana Whitfield's 14:09 question, the transcript reports a $225 monthly plan charge, 11 full outage days, and an agent-reported NOC lookup. Rule 3 yields a provisional **$82.50** outage-credit calculation. Rule 3 is separate from and exempt from Rule 2's cumulative $75 goodwill limit. Independent ticket verification, prior-dispute status, and fraud status remain unknown, so issuance is not authorized and the trace does not say a credit was approved or issued.

At 14:11, KB-1004 supports that an outage credit applies to the recurring plan charge; KB-1003 says taxes and surcharges are itemized separately. The sources do not say whether those items are recalculated or refunded after the credit. The answer gives that supported scope, leaves tax treatment unresolved, and escalates the unanswered part. At 14:13, Rule 3 and KB-1005 support a request deadline 30 calendar days after resolution: for the reported September 21 resolution, October 21 is the deadline and the October 5 request is timely.

Rule 1 applies when a customer disputes a specific invoice line item. Rule 3 governs a service-outage credit without importing Rule 1's statement-date window. Rule 2 is evaluated separately when its own confirmed-billing-error conditions are relevant. Rule 3's exception to Rule 2's $75 cap does not waive cross-cutting Rule 4 or Rule 5 review: outage credits above $300, outages above 30 days, unverifiable tickets, repeat disputes within 90 days, and fraud indicators have their own review gates. Unknown authorization checks stay pending. Ambiguous “coverage” questions ask whether the customer means an outage or Device Protection; unsupported topics are clarified, partially answered, escalated, or declined.

"Grounded" means the cited evidence supports the claim's meaning and any derived amount or date; "partially grounded" marks a supported portion while stating what remains unknown; "unsupported" claims are not rendered as affirmative facts. The pre-change baseline ran **102 tests**; the finalized suite runs **126**, including policy boundaries, the five reviewed failures, unrelated evidence, missing support, and attempted policy overrides. These tests measure targeted behavior, not zero hallucinations or complete compliance. Broader evaluation should measure rule-selection accuracy, grounded-claim precision, unsupported consequential-claim rate, escalation recall, unnecessary escalation, and answer coverage on versioned held-out cases.

The prototype has no live NOC, billing, or account integration and cannot issue credits. The KB header says nine articles, but ten headings exist; ingestion uses those headings without changing the source. No LLM or external dependency is required.
